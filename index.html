Clinical Glossary

1. Core Sentiment Metrics (Single-Day Snapshot)

  • Weighted Net Sentiment Score (NSS_Raw): A raw numerical value quantifying the absolute directional conviction of retail sentiment. It assigns double weight to "Strong" sentiment markers (Strong Bull/Strong Bear) to prioritize high-conviction opinions over casual mentions.
  • Normalized NSS (NSS_Norm): The NSS scaled by the total number of stock mentions. This generates a standardized value between -2.0 (maximum bearishness) and +2.0 (maximum bullishness), enabling direct comparison between low-visibility and high-visibility equities. Calculated independently for Short-Term (ST) and Long-Term (LT) horizons.
  • Bull-to-Bear Ratio (BBR): A pure volume metric representing the proportion of positive sentiment to negative sentiment, discarding neutral data. A value above 1.0 indicates bullish dominance; below 1.0 indicates bearish dominance.
  • Horizon Divergence Delta (HDD): The calculated difference between the Short-Term Normalized NSS and the Long-Term Normalized NSS (ST_NSS_Norm - LT_NSS_Norm). It measures the spread between immediate retail reaction (hype/panic) and underlying fundamental conviction.
  • Polarization Index: A metric measuring the concentration of extreme sentiment (Strong Bull + Strong Bear) relative to total discussion volume across both time horizons. High values indicate highly contested, volatile equities; low values indicate consensus or a lack of aggressive directional bias.

2. Time-Series Metrics (Historical 21-Day Rolling Math)

  • Relative Mention Volume (RVol): A measure of current attention versus the historical baseline. Calculated as today's Total Mentions divided by the 20-Day Simple Moving Average (SMA) of Total Mentions. A value of 2.0 indicates chatter is currently double the normal rate.
  • Sentiment Velocity (NSS Delta): Measures the speed and direction of sentiment shifts. Calculated as today's ST Normalized NSS minus the 3-Day SMA of the ST Normalized NSS. Positive values indicate a sudden influx of bullishness; negative values indicate sudden panic.
  • Polarization Z-Score: A statistical measure of crowd division anomaly. Calculates how many standard deviations today's Polarization Index is from the 20-day mean. A Z-score > 2.0 indicates an unusual spike in disagreement, often preceding severe volatility or binary events.
  • Setup Consistency (Duration): An integer counter tracking the number of consecutive days a stock has maintained its current Trading_Setup tag. Used to filter out 1-day noise anomalies and identify sustained, proven conviction.

3. Categorical & Qualitative Definitions

  • Value_Score: A continuous metric ranging from -1.0 to 1.0 that quantifies the qualitative nature of a stock's social volume.
    • Calculation: It is derived from a weighted average of subreddit mentions. Professional and fundamental subreddits (e.g., SecurityAnalysis, ValueInvesting) carry positive weights (up to +10), while speculative and derivative-focused subreddits (e.g., wallstreetbets, Daytrading) carry negative weights (down to -10).
    • Interpretation: A score approaching 1.0 indicates volume driven entirely by fundamental analysis. A score approaching -1.0 indicates volume driven entirely by retail speculation and hype. A score near 0 indicates mixed or neutral origin.
  • Selected_Seeds: A hierarchical classification system that identifies the trajectory and origin of social volume based on a 3-day rolling window.
    • New Seeds: Symbols registering positive mention volume on the current date, but which had exactly zero mentions over the preceding 3-day window. Indicates absolute initial discovery.
    • Viral Seeds: Symbols where current total mention volume exceeds the 3-day rolling average, the volume is net-bullish (Bull mentions > Bear mentions), and the symbol had existing baseline mentions in the prior 3 days. Indicates sudden, rapid acceleration in social interest.
    • Steady Hype Seeds: Symbols maintaining an uninterrupted presence (minimum 3-day daily mentions > 0) where the Value_Score remains weighted toward speculative subreddits (Score < -0.1). Indicates persistent retail gambling interest.
    • Steady Value Seeds: Symbols maintaining an uninterrupted presence (minimum 3-day daily mentions > 0) where the Value_Score remains weighted toward fundamental subreddits (Score > 0.1). Indicates sustained fundamental due diligence.
    • Growing Hype Seeds: Symbols where the current mention volume explicitly originating from speculative subreddits exceeds the 3-day rolling average for those specific subreddits, accompanied by a net-bullish sentiment.
    • Growing Value Seeds: Symbols where the current mention volume explicitly originating from fundamental subreddits exceeds the 3-day rolling average for those specific subreddits, accompanied by a net-bullish sentiment.
    • Dying Seeds: Symbols where the current bearish mention volume strictly exceeds the 3-day rolling average of bearish mentions. Indicates accelerating negative sentiment, regardless of the underlying subreddit origin.
